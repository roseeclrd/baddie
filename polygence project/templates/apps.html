Apps that might help you

WalkSafe+

https://walksafe.io/

WalkSafe+ is a personal safety app that lets you share your journey, alert trusted friends, and view safe spaces on a map.

  • Journey sharing or “Follow Me” feature
  • SOS button to alert trusted contacts
  • Interactive map of “Safe Spaces” trained venues
  • Community incident reporting

Shake2Safety

https://shake2safety.com/

Shake2Safety is a personal safety app that helps users quickly alert trusted contacts in emergency situations by simply shaking their phone.

  • Shake-to-activate emergency alert
  • Instant SOS message to emergency contacts
  • Live GPS location sharing
  • Audio recording during emergencies
  • One-tap access to police and help services

Hollie Guard

https://hollieguard.com/

Hollie Guard offers powerful alert features, evidence gathering, and journey monitoring to keep you safe.

  • Hold button to alert + start video/audio recording
  • Meeting Timer — if you don’t check out, alert is triggered
  • Journey feature to monitor travel from A to B
  • Optional professional monitoring (UK) via Hollie Guard Extra

Ellie’s Free Safety App

https://svfreenyc.org/app-elles-free-safety-app/

Ellie’s Free Safety App by SVFREENYC helps women and survivors access immediate support, resources, and alerts.

  • Emergency call features
  • Resource list for housing, legal help
  • Safe check-ins with trusted contacts
  • Support from survivor-focused network

eBodyGuard

https://ebodyguard.org/safety-app-for-women/

eBodyGuard is a comprehensive safety app for women that connects to first responders, records evidence, and preserves data securely.

  • SOS alert to 911 or trusted contacts
  • Voice activation or one-tap emergency
  • Record audio and video to secure cloud
  • Live location sharing and high-precision GPS

I’M SAFE

App Store

I’M SAFE is also available on the App Store, bringing the same safety network and SOS features to iOS.

  • Create private Bubbles to share live status
  • Discreet SOS or fake call to alert others
  • Record voice, location, and context during alert
  • High privacy ‒ no ads or data selling
