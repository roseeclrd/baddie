Websites that might help you

Women Helping Women

https://womenhelpingwomen.org/

Women Helping Women is a nonprofit organization based in Cincinnati, Ohio, dedicated to preventing gender-based violence and empowering survivors. The organization offers:

  • 24/7 crisis intervention services
  • Helpline services
  • Legal advocacy
  • Housing assistance
  • Safety planning

In addition to supporting survivors directly, they focus on prevention and education by teaching communities and students about healthy relationships and how to recognize signs of abuse.

Women’s Safety

https://womens-safety.com/

Women’s Safety is an online platform created to help women protect themselves and understand the risks of gender-based violence. The website provides:

  • Safety guides
  • Practical self-protection tips
  • Advice based on research
  • Information about warning signs of abuse
  • Articles about stalking, coercive control, and online safety

Moreover, Women’s Safety focuses on helping women recognize dangerous situations early and learn how to stay safe in everyday life.

Women Against Abuse

https://www.womenagainstabuse.org/

Women Against Abuse is a nonprofit organization based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, focused on supporting survivors of intimate partner violence. The organization offers:

  • Emergency safe housing / shelter
  • Legal advocacy and representation
  • 24-hour domestic violence hotline
  • Counseling and crisis support
  • Community education and prevention programs

Their mission is to provide compassionate support for survivors and lead the fight to end domestic violence through advocacy and education.

The Hotline

https://www.thehotline.org/

The Hotline is a national domestic violence hotline in the United States providing confidential support to anyone experiencing relationship abuse. They offer:

  • 24/7 phone and chat support
  • Crisis intervention
  • Safety planning
  • Help finding shelters and services
  • Support for friends and family

Their mission is to ensure that every person has access to safe, respectful, and supportive help when facing abuse.

DomesticShelters.org

https://www.domesticshelters.org/

DomesticShelters.org is an online platform that helps survivors of domestic violence find safe shelters and support programs across the U.S. and Canada. The website offers:

  • A directory of local domestic violence shelters
  • Crisis helplines and hotlines
  • Safety planning resources
  • Articles on identifying abuse
  • Guidance for friends and family of survivors

In addition to helping survivors directly, DomesticShelters.org educates communities about healthy relationships and how to recognize warning signs of abuse.

WomanAid Help

https://www.womanaid.help/

WomanAid Help is an online platform providing step-by-step guidance and support for women facing abuse. The website offers:

  • Self-help plans for domestic, workplace, and school abuse
  • Advice from trained social service professionals
  • Safety tips and strategies for everyday situations
  • Educational resources for survivors and allies
  • Helplines and contact info for local support

WomanAid Help focuses on empowering women with knowledge and practical tools to stay safe and recover from abuse.
